# 1. Use Cases

## UC-001 Student Registration

**Primary Actor:** Student

**Description:**  
A student creates a new account to access the Physics Learning Platform.

---

## UC-002 Student Login

**Primary Actor:** Student

**Description:**  
A student logs into the platform to access learning resources and practice questions.

---

## UC-003 Teacher Registration

**Primary Actor:** Teacher

**Description:**  
A teacher registers an account and submits the required information for teacher verification.

---

## UC-004 Teacher Verification

**Primary Actor:** Teacher

**Description:**  
A teacher verifies their email address or mobile phone number and awaits approval before accessing teacher-only features.

---

## UC-005 Teacher Login

**Primary Actor:** Teacher

**Description:**  
A verified teacher logs into the platform to access teacher features.

---

## UC-006 Generate MCQ Assessment

**Primary Actor:** Teacher

**Description:**  
A teacher generates a multiple-choice assessment by selecting an educational level, topic, and number of questions.

---

## UC-007 Preview and Download Assessment

**Primary Actor:** Teacher

**Description:**  
A teacher previews, prints, or downloads a generated assessment together with its answer key.

---

## UC-008 Download Worksheet

**Primary Actor:** Teacher

**Description:**  
A teacher searches for, previews, and downloads PDF worksheets organized by educational level and topic.

---

## UC-009 Student Practice

**Primary Actor:** Student

**Description:**  
A student practices Physics multiple-choice questions one at a time and receives immediate feedback.

---

## UC-010 Teacher Practice

**Primary Actor:** Teacher

**Description:**  
A teacher accesses the student practice module to experience the platform from a student's perspective.

---

## UC-011 Submit Feedback

**Primary Actor:** Teacher, Student

**Description:**  
A user submits feedback, reports an issue, or suggests an improvement to the platform.
