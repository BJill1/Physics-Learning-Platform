
The Physics Learning Platform will be developed and deployed incrementally. Each milestone will deliver a complete, functional version of the platform.

#### Milestone 1 – Physics Resource Library

* User registration and authentication.
* Role-based access control.
* Physics notes organized by educational level and topic.
* Downloadable PDF worksheets.
* Search and navigation of learning resources.
* Initial public deployment.

#### Milestone 2 – AI Worksheet and MCQ Generator

* AI-assisted generation of multiple-choice questions.
* Teacher selection of topic, difficulty level, and number of questions.
* Printable worksheets with answer keys.
* Deployment of the second platform release.

#### Milestone 3 – Student Practice Platform

* Interactive multiple-choice practice.
* One-question-at-a-time learning mode.
* Immediate feedback and explanations.
* Student score recording and progress tracking.
* Deployment of the third platform release.

#### Milestone 4 – AI Physics Tutor

* AI-generated explanations for Physics concepts.
* Simplified concept explanations.
* Additional worked examples.
* Interactive question-and-answer support.
* Deployment of the fourth platform release.

#### Milestone 5 – Teacher Dashboard

* Class management.
* Assignment creation.
* Student performance monitoring.
* Progress reports and learning analytics.
* Deployment of the fifth platform release.

#### Milestone 6 – School Timetable Generator

* Teacher, class, and subject management.
* Constraint-based timetable generation.
* Conflict detection and timetable optimization.
* Printable school timetables.
* Deployment of the sixth platform release.

#### Milestone 7 – Advanced AI Learning Platform

* Personalized learning recommendations.
* AI-assisted study plans.
* Intelligent revision support.
* Advanced analytics and reporting.
* Production-ready platform release.
