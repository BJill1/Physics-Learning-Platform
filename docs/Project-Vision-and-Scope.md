# Physics Learning Platform

## Business Requirement
Physics teachers spend considerable time preparing lesson materials, 
creating assessments, organizing teaching resources, and managing classroom activities. 
Students often have limited access to structured learning resources and opportunities for 
continuous practice outside the classroom. The proposed platform shall provide digital teaching resources, 
AI-assisted assessment generation, interactive learning tools, and classroom management features 
to improve teaching efficiency and enhance student learning outcomes.

## Problem Statement
Many Physics teachers spend significant time preparing lesson notes, creating worksheets,
developing assessment questions, and organizing teaching materials. These tasks are often
performed manually using multiple software tools, resulting in duplicated effort and reduced 
time for classroom instruction. In many schools, particularly in Cameroon, timetables are also 
prepared manually, making the process time-consuming and susceptible to scheduling conflicts.

Students also face challenges accessing well-organized Physics learning resources and receiving 
sufficient practice with immediate feedback. Learning materials are often scattered across different 
sources, and opportunities for personalized practice and self-assessment are limited.

There is a need for an integrated digital platform that centralizes Physics learning resources, 
assists teachers in preparing instructional materials and assessments, supports interactive student 
learning, and simplifies school timetable generation. Such a platform will improve teaching efficiency, 
enhance student learning outcomes, and reduce the administrative workload associated with Physics education.

## Project Objectives

1. Provide a centralized repository of well-organized Physics notes and downloadable worksheets arranged by topic and educational level.
1. Enable teachers to generate high-quality multiple-choice questions based on selected topics, difficulty levels, and the desired number of questions.
1. Provide students with an interactive learning environment where they can practise Physics questions, receive immediate feedback, and monitor their learning progress.
1. Develop AI-assisted tools that help teachers prepare instructional materials and assessments more efficiently.
1. Provide schools with a timetable generation tool that assists in producing conflict-free teaching schedules for teachers, classes, and subjects.
1. Design the platform using modern software engineering principles to ensure maintainability, scalability, security, and ease of future enhancement.
1. Deploy the application incrementally, with each milestone delivering a functional and usable version of the platform.
## Target Users
4.1 Physics Teachers

Physics teachers are the primary users of the platform. They will be able to access teaching resources, download worksheets, generate multiple-choice questions, create assessments, monitor student performance, and generate school timetables where applicable.

4.2 Students

Students will use the platform to access Physics notes, practise multiple-choice questions, receive immediate feedback on their answers, track their learning progress, and prepare for examinations through interactive learning activities.

4.3 School Administrators

School administrators will use the timetable generation module to create and manage school timetables, maintain teacher and class information, and oversee timetable scheduling for their institutions.

4.4 Platform Administrator

The platform administrator will be responsible for managing the system, including user accounts, Physics content, worksheets, question banks, AI configuration, and overall platform maintenance.

4.5 Future Users

Future versions of the platform may support additional users, including parents, school owners, curriculum developers, educational institutions, and examination bodies, as the platform expands.

## Project Scope

The project will deliver an AI-powered Physics learning platform through multiple incremental releases. The scope includes:

- Physics learning resources
- Downloadable worksheets
- AI-assisted MCQ generation
- Interactive student practice
- AI tutoring
- Teacher management tools
- School timetable generation

Detailed implementation milestones are described in the Project Roadmap document.
